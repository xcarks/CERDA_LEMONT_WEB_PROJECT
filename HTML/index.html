Welcome to EFREI Paris

Founded in 1936, Efrei is celebrating 90 years as a leading French engineering school specializing in computer science and digital technologies. With campuses in Villejuif (Grand Paris) and Bordeaux, Efrei is considered one of the best private engineering schools in France.

With over 17,200 alumni, 2,000 corporate partners, and 93 partner universities worldwide, Efrei connects students directly to the professional world from day one.

Our Computer Science programs train the next generation of engineers across key specializations including software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data science, and cloud computing. Students benefit from modern Fablabs, research laboratories, coworking spaces, and real-world projects developed with industry partners.

Our engineering degree is accredited by the Commission des Titres d'Ingénieur (CTI), and our Tech & Digital programs are recognized by the French State through RNCP certification guaranteeing strong academic standards and international recognition.

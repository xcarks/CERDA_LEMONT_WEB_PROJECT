Welcome to EFREI Paris

Founded over 90 years ago, EFREI Paris is a leading French engineering school specializing in computer science and digital technologies. Located in Villejuif (Paris) and Bordeaux, EFREI is recognized as the #1 private computer engineering school in France by L'Étudiant ranking.

With over 17,200 alumni and partnerships with 2,000+ companies, EFREI connects students directly to the professional world from day one.

The Computer Science Department trains the next generation of engineers in software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data science, and cloud computing. Students benefit from modern Fablabs, research labs, and real-world projects with industry partners.

Our CTI-accredited programs combine strong theory with hands-on practice, preparing graduates for top positions in the global tech industry.
